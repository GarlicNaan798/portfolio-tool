Grinold (1989), "The Fundamental Law of Active Management", *Journal of
Portfolio Management*; extended in Grinold & Kahn, *Active Portfolio
Management*.

Information ratio is approximately information coefficient times the square
root of breadth, where breadth counts *independent* bets per year. Correlated
bets push effective breadth well below the nominal count.

**For us:** the framework behind [[phase-c-breadth-was-not-the-constraint]].
Our measured IR ran about 40% of the law's prediction on correlated large
caps, which is the expected direction. It also explains the gap to the
published literature: papers apply a small IC across 1000+ names; we had 22.
