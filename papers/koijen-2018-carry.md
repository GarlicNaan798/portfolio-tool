Koijen, Moskowitz, Pedersen & Vrugt (2018), "Carry", *Journal of Financial
Economics* 127(2), 197-225.

Generalises FX carry to any asset: expected return decomposes into carry - an
ex-ante, model-free characteristic - plus expected price appreciation. Carry
predicts returns in global equities, global bonds, currencies, commodities, US
Treasuries, credit and equity index options. Carry strategies load on global
recession, liquidity and volatility risk, but none fully explains the premium.

**For us:** the second untested futures factor, and computable from data we
can obtain. Commodity carry reads off the futures curve
(contango/backwardation), bond carry off the yield-curve slope, FX carry off
the rate differential.
