Multiple 2024-2026 sources: "Momentum Trading in Cryptocurrencies" (VU
journals); "Systematic Trend-Following with Adaptive Portfolio Construction"
(arXiv); "A Trend Factor for the Cross Section of Cryptocurrency Returns"
(JFQA).

Time-series momentum reported around 32% annual; one adaptive trend system
claims Sharpe 2.41 across 2022-2024. The effect concentrates in smaller, less
liquid tokens and in high-uncertainty regimes. Notably TSMOM lost money through
the 2022-23 drawdown while cross-sectional held up, being market-neutral.

**For us: excluded by user direction - no crypto.** Filed because it is the
one market where the literature says the effect remains strong, so the
exclusion is a deliberate constraint rather than an oversight.
