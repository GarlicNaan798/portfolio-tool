Pan (2004), "A Swingtum Theory of Intelligent Finance for Swing Trading and
Momentum Trading", IWIF-1 Melbourne. In the repo root as
`mining_associated_implication_networks_computational_intermarket_analysis.pdf`.

Proposes Super Bayesian Influence Networks over Probability Ensembles of
Neural Networks, drawing on multifractals, log-periodic power laws and
physical cycles, plus intermarket influence via an Asymmetrical Dependence
Test.

**Not actionable here.** It is a taxonomy and research programme, not a rule
set: no reproducible parameters, and the single performance claim (daily
direction of the Australian All Ordinaries) carries no figures.

The one contribution worth keeping is a clean definition:

    swing     bounded by a range or channel; confirmed by a SUCCESSFUL test
              of a key level
    momentum  half-open, no target; confirmed by a FAILED test - a breakout

Everything built in this repo so far trades the failed test. The successful
test - fading a level that holds - is the untested half. That framing is
what [[abrams-2010-mr-swing]] operationalises.
