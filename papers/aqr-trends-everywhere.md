Babu, Levine, Ooi, Pedersen & Stamelos, "Trends Everywhere", *Journal of
Investment Management*.

Applies trend to a much wider set of markets, including less liquid ones, and
finds the effect persists. Diversification across many markets is a major
driver of the strategy's risk-adjusted return.

**For us:** argues our 31-market Phase E universe is on the small side, and
that breadth of *markets* does help here - unlike breadth of correlated
single stocks in [[phase-c-breadth-was-not-the-constraint]] - because futures
markets across asset classes are genuinely less correlated with each other.
