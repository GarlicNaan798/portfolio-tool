Hurst, Ooi & Pedersen (2017), "A Century of Evidence on Trend-Following
Investing", *Journal of Portfolio Management*.

Simulated 1-, 3- and 12-month trend across equities, bonds, commodities and
currencies back to 1880. Positive in every decade, with the strongest
performance during large equity drawdowns - the "crisis alpha" claim.

**For us:** the long-horizon case that trend is not a data-mining artifact.
It does not contradict [[trend-following-decayed-after-2009]]; a century of
positive decades is entirely compatible with one weak recent one.
