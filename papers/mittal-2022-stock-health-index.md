Mittal & Nagpal (2022), "Predicting a reliable stock for mid and long term
investment", *Journal of King Saud University - CIS* 34. In the repo root as
`Research paper.pdf`.

A regression model over fundamental features yields a health index, classified
into fuzzy sets that generate buy/hold/sell advice. Aimed at Indian retail
investors choosing among roughly 2000 NSE listings.

**For us: unused, deliberately.** It ranks stocks cross-sectionally on
fundamentals; we trade one instrument, or a small futures basket, on price
data. Filed so the decision not to use it is recorded rather than
re-litigated every time the papers are reviewed.
