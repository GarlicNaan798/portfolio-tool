Hurst, Ooi & Pedersen, "Demystifying Managed Futures", *Journal of Investment
Management*.

Simple 1-, 3- and 12-month trend signals on a diversified futures book explain
the bulk of managed futures index returns, implying the industry's aggregate
return is a systematic and cheaply replicable premium rather than manager
skill.

**For us:** justifies benchmarking our futures work against a plain trend rule
rather than trying to be clever, and supports the Phase E design directly.
