Lemperiere, Deremble, Seager, Potters & Bouchaud (2014), "Two Centuries of
Trend Following", CFM.

Extends trend evidence to spot series from 1800. Stable across both time and
asset class. The authors argue the t-statistic makes it one of the strongest
anomalies in finance and hard to reconcile with efficient markets.

**For us:** strengthens the prior that the effect is real, which is exactly
why [[phase-e-validated-the-engine]] was worth running as a positive control.
A tool that cannot find this is broken.
