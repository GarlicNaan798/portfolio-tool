Asness, Moskowitz & Pedersen (2013), "Value and Momentum Everywhere",
*Journal of Finance* 68(3).

Value and momentum premia appear in US, UK, European and Japanese stocks,
equity index futures, government bonds, currencies and commodity futures.
Momentum correlates positively across asset classes and value likewise - but
**value and momentum are negatively correlated with each other**, both within
and across classes. The combined global portfolio has a far higher Sharpe than
either sleeve alone.

**For us:** the direct fix for [[phase-a-blend-failed-on-correlation]]. Our
three supposedly different sleeves were 0.67-0.69 correlated to SPY. Value is
the negatively-correlated ingredient we never had.
